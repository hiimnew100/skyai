# Small business frontend work

I offer two fixed-scope services for small business websites:

- **$50 — focused repair:** refresh up to three adjacent content blocks on one page using your approved copy, improve the mobile layout and one contact action, and deliver the source/patch with one revision.
- **$100 — one-page site:** responsive page with up to five sections, a WhatsApp inquiry flow, source files, setup notes and one revision.

The delivery target is 24 hours after the approved content/assets and source or a safe staging environment are available. Hosting, domain, paid services, backend integrations and sales guarantees are outside the scope. We agree on the final scope, currency, timing and payment route before work begins. No production passwords or secrets are needed.
